Still writing in Markdown, but the results feel too plain? Want rich HTML results, but writing HTML feels too complex?

MDS is here to clear that friction. Keep writing Markdown — get web-grade results.

Write Markdown as usual, add a few semantic blocks. Layout, motion, and interaction go to the MDS renderer, and you get rich, polished pages that feel like HTML.

Let's look at the authoring flow.
Start with plain Markdown.
Headings, lists, quotes — the MDS renderer turns them into beautiful HTML.
Then write a hero.
A title, one line of copy, and a button.
The MDS renderer turns it into a real page right away.
Of course, you can also write controls the way you would in HTML.
Buttons, sliders, switches — the MDS renderer renders those too.

There are several default themes to choose from.
default, light, dark, and rich — or build your own if you want something different.
